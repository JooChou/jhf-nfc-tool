ENGLISH

Your tags, in detail.

Read and inspect

View UID, manufacturer and memory information. Read all memory blocks or inspect a selected range in HEX, ASCII or decimal.

Write with checks

Write selected blocks or byte ranges, with UID checks and read-back verification to help check the operation.

What you need

An NFC-capable iPhone running iOS 16 or later and a compatible ISO15693 tag. The current app does not provide general support for every RFID or NFC tag technology. Available memory areas and commands depend on the tag and Core NFC.

Writing or clearing can overwrite tag data. Use tags you are authorised to modify, check the UID and keep a copy of important data.

Quick start

  1. Open Tag Info or Read All.
  2. Tap the read button and wait for the system scanning sheet.
  3. Hold a compatible tag near the top of your iPhone and keep it steady until the read finishes.
  4. For range operations, confirm the start block or byte offset and count before continuing.

Help and troubleshooting

The tag cannot be read

Check that the tag uses ISO15693. Remove a case that interferes with scanning, keep only one tag nearby, and hold it steadily at the top of the phone. A Simulator cannot perform real NFC reads. Protected or unsupported tag commands may fail.

A full read stops partway through

Keep the tag in the same position until the scan finishes. Move away from metal surfaces or other tags and retry. Some protected areas may not be readable. A partial result is not a completed full read.

Result Forwarding cannot connect

Use an accessible TCP server and verify its address and port. Allow local-network access when connecting to a local server. Check the server listener and network settings. Results are sent to the server you enter; verify that you trust it.

常见问题

标签无法识别怎么办？

确认标签协议为 ISO15693，移开其他标签和金属干扰，必要时取下影响感应的保护壳，将标签贴近手机顶部。模拟器不能进行真实 NFC 扫描；受保护区域或不支持的指令可能无法读取。

整块读取中途断开怎么办？

保持标签位置，等待扫描完成后再移开；远离金属和其他标签再重试。部分受保护区域可能无法读取，部分读取结果不等于全部读取完成。

TCP 结果转发连接失败怎么办？

检查服务器地址、端口、监听服务及网络可达性。连接局域网服务器时允许本地网络权限。数据会发到您填写的服务器，请自行确认其可靠性。

如何恢复购买？

在 App“支持”页面点击“恢复购买”，使用原购买时的 Apple 账户，并允许连接 Apple 服务。已到期的订阅不会恢复为有效订阅。

Privacy Policy

JHF NFC Tool • Updated October 6, 2026

NFC data

The app reads and writes ISO15693 tags at your request. Tag identifiers (UID), manufacturer information, memory information and block contents are processed on your device. The current app does not include developer-operated data upload, advertising, analytics or account registration. The developer also cannot use the tag data read by the app for other purposes.

Local preferences

Language, byte display order, and the server address and port you enter are saved in the app’s local preferences. These settings may be included in backups managed by your operating system. Tag results are held in memory rather than saved to a tag history database.

Result Forwarding

Basic NFC functions work offline. Result Forwarding, purchases and purchase restoration require a network connection. To use Result Forwarding, you need to configure a TCP server. Subsequent tag information and full/range results are automatically sent to that server, including UID, raw tag contents, request identifiers and timestamps, until you disconnect or the connection ends. This may include partial full-read results and read-back verification results from range write and clear operations. This feature is intended for connecting to servers on your local network. The actual recipient is determined by the address you enter. You need to verify the server’s reliability.

Retention and deletion

Reading results are cleared as operations and pages reset or when the app process ends. Delete the app to remove its local app data; operating system backups are managed separately.

Purchases

Advanced feature purchases and purchase restoration use Apple StoreKit and require a connection to Apple services. Apple processes payment information; the developer does not receive your bank card details. The app verifies purchase transactions on your device to determine access to paid features.

Policy updates and contact

Changes to data handling will be reflected in an updated policy. Developer: JooChao. Contact email for privacy questions: zzyself@163.com.

隐私政策

JHF NFC Tool • 更新日期：2026 年 10 月 6 日

NFC 数据

应用按您的操作读取和写入 ISO15693 标签，在设备上处理标签 UID、厂商信息、存储信息和块内容。当前应用不集成开发者运营的数据上传、广告、统计分析或账户注册，同时开发者也无法利用读取的标签数据进行其他作用。

本地设置

语言、字节显示顺序及您输入的服务器地址和端口保存在应用本地设置中。这些设置可能包含在操作系统管理的备份中。标签结果保存在内存中，当前没有标签历史数据库。

结果转发

基础 NFC 功能可离线使用；结果转发、购买和恢复购买需要联网。当您使用“结果转发”功能时，需要配置一个TCP服务器，后续标签信息、整块和范围结果会自动发送到该服务器，包含UID、原始标签内容、请求标识和时间戳，直至您断开或连接结束。这可能包含整块读取的部分结果以及范围写入、清空的回读校验结果。该功能适用于连接局域网服务器，实际接收方由您输入的地址决定，您需要对服务器的可靠性做验证。

保留与删除

读取结果随操作和页面重置或应用进程结束而清除。删除应用可移除其本地应用数据，系统备份需另行管理。

购买与恢复

高级功能购买及恢复购买使用 Apple StoreKit，需要连接 Apple 服务。支付资料由 Apple 处理，开发者不会收到您的银行卡信息。应用在设备上验证购买交易，以确认付费功能的使用权限。
